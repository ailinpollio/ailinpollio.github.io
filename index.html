About

Short bio

I am a PhD candidate at the Laboratoire d’Informatique et Systèmes (LIS) in Marseille, France, under the supervision of Marvin Lavechin and Magalie Ochs. Before that, I was part of:

  • GIPSA-lab in Grenoble as a CNRS Research Engineer, working on end-to-end dysarthric speech reconstruction with Thomas Hueber and Olivier Perrotin.
  • The Computational Speech Group at the University of Eastern Finland as a Machine Learning Research Intern, under the supervision of Tomi Kinnunen.

I also worked in the private sector at:

  • Virtuosis AI as an AI Research Engineer, developing classifiers to detect various diseases affecting speech using vocal biomarkers.
  • Callyope as an NLP Engineer, improving clinical speech-to-text systems.

Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of speech processing, machine learning, and cognition. I am interested in using computational representations of speech to better understand how language is encoded, processed, and acquired, both in typical contexts and in the presence of speech and language impairments.
